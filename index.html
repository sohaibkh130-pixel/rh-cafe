Our story

Say little, cook well.

Café RH started from one idea: a good evening doesn't need to announce itself. No tableside performance, no twelve-course marathon — a small room, a menu that changes with what the morning market gave us, and dishes that trust the ingredient more than the technique.

There's nowhere to hide behind three ingredients — so we make sure all three are worth eating.— The kitchen, Café RH

The menu is set each morning, not printed for the season. Every plate leaves the kitchen at the pace of the table it's going to — dinner here tends to run long, and that's by design.
